FAQ

Questions planners ask us

How fast can you deliver?

Urgent orders can be made and delivered within a day. Tell us your event date when you message us and we'll confirm the timeline.

We're an event company from outside Udaipur. Can we work with you remotely?

Yes. Send us the design, size, venue and event date on WhatsApp. We share a preview for approval, build the sign in Udaipur and deliver it to your venue.

Do you deliver to hotels and wedding venues?

Yes, we deliver to venues across Udaipur. Share the venue name and the time you need the sign there.

Can you make several signs for one wedding?

Yes. Many weddings need signs for each function, like haldi, mehendi, sangeet and reception. We can make all of them together.

Can you make signs in Hindi or with a custom logo?

Yes. We make signs in Hindi and English, in any font and colour, and can recreate your logo in neon. See our gallery for examples.

Can I see the design before you make it?

Yes, we share a design preview for approval before we start building. You can also visit our workshop in Shobhagpura.

Event in Udaipur coming up?

Send us your idea and event date. We'll reply with a design and a price.
